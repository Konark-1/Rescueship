## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & 50-Brand Selection Criteria

### A. The Core Target ICP Archetype
RescueShip drives maximum unit economic leverage for fast-growing Indian Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) brands operating on Shopify or WooCommerce that meet the following parameters:

| Criteria Parameter | Target Threshold | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Order Volume** | **500 – 5,000 orders/month** | High enough for RTO losses to burn real profit (₹1.5L – ₹12L/month), yet lean enough to lack an in-house 20-seat call center. |
| **COD Order Share** | **50% – 75%** | Typical for Indian Tier-2/3 consumer demand where impulse buying triggers high delivery refusal rates. |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | **₹800 – ₹3,500** | Healthy gross margins where a ₹200–₹250 sunk freight loss wipes out 40%–100% of order profitability. |
| **Primary Verticals** | Fashion, Footwear, Clean Cosmetics & Beauty, Personal Care, Activewear | High impulse purchase categories prone to buyer regret, size indecision, and delivery delays. |
| **Logistics Stack** | Shiprocket, Delhivery Direct, BlueDart, ClickPost, NimbusPost, Shadowfax | Pre-built carrier adapters allow zero-code deployment in under 5 minutes. |
| **Decision Makers** | Founder / CEO, Head of E-Commerce, VP of Logistics & Supply Chain, Growth Lead | Direct line to the P&L; acutely sensitive to marketing payback and net profit bleed. |
